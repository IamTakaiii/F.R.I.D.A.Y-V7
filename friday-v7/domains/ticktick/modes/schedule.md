# TickTick — schedule

Daily timeblocks. Preview before writing start/end.

Order: fixed/overdue → due today → current queue → recurring Areas due today. Require Estimate; ask or leave unscheduled — do not guess. Preserve fixed blocks.

Show timeline + overflow. Do not overwrite a hard due date just to encode a block. Meetings outside TickTick are invisible unless the user names them. Do not skip breaks or cross the working window without approval. Re-run updates existing tasks; never duplicate schedule tasks.
