Presentable export from Intent + Design. Not a second source of truth.

## Require

`intent.md` + `design.md` (and ADR links) or user paste. Missing → say so; do not invent contracts.

Purpose/scope · Bottom line (what ships) · Behavior & contracts · Failure modes · Out of scope · Open questions · Sources.

Label gaps `Open` or `Assumed`. Prefatory line: `Source of truth: [[intent]] · [[design]]`.
