# Perf standard

`/fr-test` when the run is bench or load. Compare **measured** numbers to Design quality bounds.

No bound in Design → stop and update Design, or user-owned `Assumed` (owner + date). A ship-cited perf report cannot **PASS** on Assumed alone.

Persist `quality-report` `kind: perf`. Init tooling stays in `modes/test.md` (propose before install).
