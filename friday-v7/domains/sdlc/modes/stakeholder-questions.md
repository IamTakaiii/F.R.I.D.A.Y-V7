# SDLC — stakeholder questions

Create a questionnaire when the current user cannot own a needed fact or decision. Writes: `questionnaire`.

**Interview the send, not the subject.** Ask only what the current user can answer:

1. Recipient: role, expertise, relationship, and what they know that we do not.
2. Return: exact facts/decisions needed and what the answers will unlock.

Then write questions across that knowledge gap.

## Document bar

- Purpose and decision riding on it.
- From · To · how answers will be used.
- One paragraph of context for someone outside this conversation.
- Deadline and rough effort; partial answers and “I don't know” are useful.
- Most important first; theme headings after a handful.
- One idea per question, with an answer stub directly below.
- `_Why this matters:_` only where misreading or a throwaway answer is likely.
- Final “What did we fail to ask?” catch-all.

Done when every needed return from step 2 maps to a question. Report the path and named recipient; do not pretend the questionnaire resolved the decision.
